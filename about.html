Prompt Ustad is built by Mudassir Khan, a web and WordPress developer. It helps you turn a rough task into a structured prompt, then review and edit each part.

Free means every feature

The studio, examples, editable blocks, local drafts, follow-up builder and text exports are free. There are no paid plans, subscriptions or paid templates.

What it can and cannot do

It uses preset rules to organise your input. It does not call an AI model, answer your question, translate your original brief or guarantee the quality of a response. You choose where to use the prompt and remain responsible for checking the result.

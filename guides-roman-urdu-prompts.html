Write in Roman Urdu, ask for an English response

Keep your original brief and tell the receiving AI how to respond.

You can describe your work in the language that feels natural to you. That does not mean your final response has to use the same language.

Separate your input from the requested response

In Prompt Ustad, the app-language switch changes the interface. The response-language dropdown adds instructions about the language the receiving AI should use. These are separate controls.

Meri home bakery ke liye 7 din ka Instagram content plan chahiye. Roz 30 minutes hain. Sirf organic posts. Jawab English mein table ki shakal mein do.

Include the details in your own words

The example identifies the business, deliverable, available time, constraint, output language and format. Those facts are more useful than a long list of expert titles.

What the builder does

The builder keeps your original brief and places it alongside structured instructions. It does not translate your Roman Urdu sentences into English and it does not call an AI model. Paste the prompt into the AI tool you choose, then check whether that tool understood the brief.

Clarify local terms

If a term has more than one meaning, explain it. Specify PKR when discussing a rupee budget, identify the intended audience and describe local ordering or delivery requirements. Do not include private customer records in an example.

Check the response

If the output changes your numbers, assumes a paid advertising budget or uses the wrong language, write a focused follow-up explaining exactly what must stay unchanged. The next guide shows a practical way to do that.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Simple by choice.

Is Prompt Ustad completely free?

Yes. Every feature in this version is free. There are no subscriptions, paid templates or locked exports.

Does it generate an AI answer?

No. It builds a structured prompt using your input and preset rules. Review the result, then copy it into your chosen AI tool. No AI API key is required here.

Can I write in Roman Urdu?

Yes. You can write your brief in English or Roman Urdu and choose the language you want the receiving AI to answer in. Your original input stays in the prompt; the builder does not translate it.

Where are my drafts saved?

In this browser’s local storage. They are not synced across devices. Clearing browser data removes them, so download important prompts.

YOUR DATA AND YOUR CHOICES

Privacy & browser storage

Updated 27 October 2026. Prompt Ustad is free to use without an account. Optional analytics helps us understand visits; it does not collect the text you write in the builder.

Your prompts stay in your browser

Prompt Ustad builds prompts using rules on your device. It does not send your brief, generated prompt, study question, feedback or saved draft to an AI service or to Google Analytics. If you copy a prompt into another service, that service handles it under its own policies.

Local drafts

Drafts, recent prompts and preferences are stored in this browser. They are not synced between devices. Clearing browser data can remove them. Back up important prompts with the studio's export controls. Avoid entering confidential information on shared devices.

Optional Google Analytics

When you select “Allow analytics”, this website loads Google Analytics 4 to measure page visits, engagement and traffic sources. Reports may include page addresses and titles, referring website domains, browser/device information, approximate geographic information and cookie identifiers. Google processes network request information to provide this service. Analytics cookies are configured with a six-month lifetime. Advertising personalisation and Google signals are not enabled by this site's tag.

We do not send builder fields, generated text, names or email addresses as analytics events. Automatic enhanced measurement is off. The page location sent by our tag excludes query strings and fragments, and the referrer is limited to its website origin.

Your choice

Before you allow analytics, the website does not load the Google Analytics tag or send measurement requests to Google. “Continue without” keeps analytics off and all free features available. Your choice is stored in this browser for up to 180 days. You can reopen “Analytics preferences” in the footer to change it. Withdrawing permission disables measurement and removes this tool's analytics cookies; previously collected data is not removed by that browser action. After re-allowing a tag that was already disabled on the same page, collection resumes on the next page load.

Aapki brief, study questions aur prompts browser mein rehte hain. Google Analytics sirf aapki ijazat ke baad visits measure karta hai. “Continue without” chunne par bhi tamam free features chalte hain. Footer se Analytics preferences dobara khol sakte hain.

Hosting and third-party services

The public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may process normal request and security information independently of the optional analytics tag. No signup, payment or advertising tracker is required to use the studio. For more information, read Google's Privacy Policy and GitHub's Privacy Statement.

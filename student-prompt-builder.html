Use AI to study with a clearer prompt

Prompt Ustad is a free AI prompt builder for students. Organise a study question in English or Roman Urdu, then copy the editable prompt to ChatGPT or another AI tool.

Built and maintained by Mudassir Khan, founder of Prompt Ustad · Updated 7 November 2026.

Prompt Ustad helps students tell an AI tool what they are trying to learn, what they already know and what kind of help they want. It structures the request; it does not solve the question, generate an answer or check facts.

Turn a vague study question into a useful request

A short request such as “Teach me algebra” gives an AI little information about your level or how you want to practise.

Teach me how to solve a two-step linear equation. Assume I know basic arithmetic. Explain each step and why it works, then give me three similar questions without the solutions. Wait for my attempt before showing answers. If I make a mistake, give me a hint about that step.

This prompt asks for an explanation and practice at a stated level. You can edit the subject, prior knowledge and format to match your course.

Example: study in Roman Urdu

If Roman Urdu is easier, keep the request in your own words and specify the kind of help you need. For example:

Mujhe photosynthesis simple Roman Urdu mein samjhao. Pehle chlorophyll, sunlight aur carbon dioxide ka matlab batao. Phir ek rozmarra ki misaal do aur 3 practice questions banao. Jawabat tab dikhana jab main apni koshish bhej doon. Agar kisi baat ka yaqeen na ho to usay fact ki tarah mat likhna.

Prompt Ustad keeps your original Roman Urdu wording. It does not translate the brief; the AI tool you choose will respond according to the language instruction you include.

Useful study prompts to try

  • Understand a concept: ask for a plain-language explanation, define important terms and include one example.
  • Practise recall: request questions first and ask the AI to wait until you answer before showing solutions.
  • Plan revision: provide your topics, available study time and exam date; ask for a realistic schedule with breaks.
  • Review notes: paste non-sensitive course notes and ask for a summary that distinguishes supplied facts from anything uncertain.

Build a study prompt with Prompt Ustad

  1. Write the subject, topic and what you want to understand.
  2. Add your current level, relevant context and any limits.
  3. Choose an output format, such as steps, examples, a quiz or a revision plan.
  4. Review and edit the assembled prompt, then copy it into your chosen AI tool.
  5. Check the response against your course material. Do not submit AI-written work as your own.

Keep your information safe

Do not include student names, passwords, private records or confidential school material in a prompt. Follow your teacher’s rules for AI use, and verify important answers with the material used in your class.

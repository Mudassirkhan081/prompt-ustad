Local drafts and guest allowance

Existing drafts, Recent prompts and preferences remain in browser storage. The guest counter and authentication session use separate pu_auth_ keys. Signing out does not erase prompts. Account deletion removes the Supabase Auth user; local prompts remain until you remove them or clear browser storage.

Browser-only generation

The app is rule-based. It does not call an AI model and does not verify AI answers. Copying text into another service is your own action and that service’s terms apply. Avoid confidential information on shared devices. Local storage is not a cloud backup.

Account and updates

Email confirmation is required. Signing up does not automatically opt you into product updates. Your update preference is stored as marketing_opt_in in Auth user metadata. There are no application database tables or prompt-content uploads.

Hosting and availability

The hosting provider and authentication provider may process normal request and security information. No analytics, advertising or third-party frontend library is added. Accounts require an internet connection; an existing stored confirmed session can continue local generation while offline. Free usage is not promised to be secure against changes to the browser.
